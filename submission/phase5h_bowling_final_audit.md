## Source audit and transformations

The source feed does not contain these fields: `total_runs`, `total_runs_external`, `is_legal_delivery`, `legal_delivery`, `bowler_conceded_runs`. The source `runs` field is supported as batter runs on ordinary rows only; absent total-run and typed-extra amounts prevent safe reconstruction of bowler-conceded runs.

Within the confirmed IPL/T20I 2022+ scope, `extras_2_runs` distribution is {0: 193979, 1: 19, 4: 16}; the same category-like `extras1_runs` label/code pattern is observed as Overstep=1, Penalty=7, byes=4, legbyes=3, other=5, wide=2, null=6. Null `extras_type` is the ordinary/no-extra category, not missing event classification. These are scoped observations; they do not resolve amount attribution.

Bowler-credit events are generated in `src/pressure_analysis.py`: `is_wicket=True` AND normalized `dismissal_type` is Bowled, Caught, LBW, Stump or Hit Wicket. Within scope, this rule identifies 9,905 events; 0 have no `bowler_id`, none occur on Overstep, and 25 credited Stump events occur on wide rows. RunOut, Retire Out and Obstructing-field dismissals are excluded. These are defensible recognized credited-event counts, but not a complete independently reconciled player bowling figures dataset. Do not equate them with all `is_wicket` events.

The processed `legal_delivery` field is derived, not raw: ordinary events are provisionally legal, wides are illegal, Overstep is inferred as no-ball, byes/leg-byes are provisionally legal, and Other/Penalty/ambiguous secondary extras remain unknown. `overid` identifies an over label; it does not give legal balls bowled. There are 262 unknown-legality rows in the IPL/T20I analysis scope, and even the known flag uses some inferred semantics. Phase 5H therefore treats legal-ball and over totals as incomplete.

The complete metric-by-format audit—including required fields, field presence, formula, denominator, coverage, eligibility, pressure relevance and limitations—is in `phase5h_bowling_metric_audit.csv`. Field-presence percentages use all scoped delivery rows; null `extras_type` is a meaningful ordinary-delivery category, and `dismissal_type` is expected only on wicket rows. The CSV distinguishes these conditional fields and reports dismissal-label coverage on wicket rows separately. `legal_delivery` and `innings_phase` are processed derivatives, not raw source columns.

The Phase 5C bowler model standardizes one recognized wicket-yield delta per format, applies harmonic exposure shrinkage and match-consistency adjustment, and labels all 414 profiles exploratory. Those proxy-derived scores are not used as submission rankings.

Pace/spin, bowling type, speed, delivery style, stroke, line/trajectory descriptors and phase are available at high rates, but are not validated pre-delivery challenge indicators or calibrated difficulty weights. Phase can control comparisons; it cannot alone define pressure.

## Final bowling decision

Keep the bowler Top 10 unresolved. The honest alternative is a non-ranked evidence table with verified chase definitions, outcomes, denominators, coverage and match-cluster uncertainty, plus the exact missing fields: complete bowler-conceded runs or typed extras with validated attribution; provider-explicit legal status for every delivery; validated wicket-credit/no-ball rules; and broader paired verified challenge states. Do not contact providers or obtain external data under this phase instruction.
